# Provenance and publication scope

- Code, skill instructions and templates were newly authored for this repository.
- Existing private workflow concepts informed the scope; their files were not copied into the release.
- All example files are synthetic. They do not reproduce research results.
- No Fortran solver, constitutive equation implementation, residual/tangent assembly, UEL property contract, real input deck, credentials, private paths or unpublished research figures are included.
- Third-party skill packages and plugin code are not redistributed.
- Hashes are generated from the bundled synthetic files.
- Source and workflow review do not establish scientific validity of a future user's simulation.
